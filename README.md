# Housing Register Eligibility Checker

![BDD tests](https://github.com/MihaylovDimi/housing-eligibility-prototype/actions/workflows/bdd-tests.yml/badge.svg)

A small Python prototype built with Behaviour-Driven Development (BDD) for SWE6301 Agile Programming, Assignment 1.

It pre-checks whether a resident meets the basic criteria to apply to a council Housing Register:

1. The applicant is at least 18 years old.
2. The applicant has lived in the borough for at least 3 years (local connection).
3. Annual household income does not exceed £30,000.

These thresholds are illustrative and do not represent the policy of any real council.
